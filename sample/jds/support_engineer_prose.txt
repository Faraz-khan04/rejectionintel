Customer Support Engineer

This posting is written as prose rather than bullets, because plenty of them are.

We need someone who has spent at least two years in a customer support or helpdesk
role and is comfortable reading logs. You should have working knowledge of SQL so you
can answer your own questions about a customer's data, familiarity with Zendesk or a
similar ticketing system, and enough Linux to be dangerous. Experience with Jira for
escalating to engineering is expected. If you have written documentation before, say
so, and if you have used Datadog to trace an issue, even better.
