Sign in

We send a one-time link. There is no password, so there is nothing to remember, reuse, or have stolen from another site.

How this is kept safe

  • The link expires in 15 minutes and works exactly once. A second click does nothing, so an old email in your inbox is not a spare key.
  • We never say whether an address has an account. The answer is identical either way — otherwise anyone could test an address and learn that person is job hunting.
  • Repeated attempts are throttled, so nobody can use this form to flood someone else's inbox.
  • Your session cookie cannot be read by scripts and you can revoke every device from here at any time.
